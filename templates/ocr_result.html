How this score is calculated

Each detected ingredient starts at 50 points. A listed skin-type match (including “all”) adds 25 points. If the type is not listed or the field is missing, the fit is Unknown and adds or subtracts no points. Benefits mentioning your selected concern add 15 points; missing concern benefits have no effect. For sensitive skin, low irritancy adds 5, medium subtracts 15, and high subtracts 30. For other profiles, medium irritancy subtracts 10 and high subtracts 20. An explicit dataset warning relevant to the profile subtracts 10. Scores are bounded to 0–100; the product score is the average. Scores of 75–100 are Recommended, 50–74 are Use with Caution, and scores below 50 are Not Recommended.

Ingredient status is Not Recommended below 50; Use with Caution when a relevant warning applies or the ingredient score is below 75; Compatible when there is a skin-type match and a score of at least 75; otherwise it is Insufficient Data / Unknown. An omitted skin type is not treated as evidence of incompatibility.

Concern-supporting benefits are matched by these listed terms: acne—acne, blemish, pore; dryness—hydrat, moistur, dry; pigmentation—pigmentation, dark spot, brighten, even tone; aging—aging, fine line, wrinkle, mature, expression line, elasticity; redness—redness, soothe, calm, irritation, rosacea. “None” has no concern match. Relevant dataset warnings are matched by profile: sensitive skin—warnings about allergy, sensitivity, irritation, dryness, purging, tingling, or stinging; acne—comedogenicity or clogged pores; dryness—drying or stripping; pigmentation—photosensitivity. A matched warning subtracts 10. The CSV pregnancy-safety rating is not scored because pregnancy is not part of the skin profile. This is an informational matching rule, not a medical assessment.
